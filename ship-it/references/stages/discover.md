# Stage 1: Discover — *Product Manager, UX Researcher*

Understand the problem before choosing a solution.

- Read the request, then the code, docs, issues, and conventions around it. Answer what you can from evidence before asking the user anything.
- Identify who has the problem and what kind of user they are (see customer personas in `references/personas.md`).
- Name the job the user is trying to get done, their current workaround, constraints, and what is explicitly out of scope.
- Produce an **evidence summary**: what you found (issues, tickets, usage patterns, feedback) and what you could not find. Turn every unvalidatable assumption into an open question for Define.

**Delegation:** on Standard and Full, run the sweep with `references/delegates/evidence-sweep.md` and work from its summary.

**Vague or open-ended requests** ("refine X", "improve Y", "I want to tweak Z"): do the discovery conversation inline — do not hand off to a separate brainstorming skill. Ask at most three focused questions at a time, each with a proposed default so the user can answer fast. Stay in this stage until you can state the problem in one sentence and say who has it.

**Gate:** You can state the problem in one sentence without mentioning a solution, and say who has it. No reviewer — this is a thinking pass, not an artifact.
